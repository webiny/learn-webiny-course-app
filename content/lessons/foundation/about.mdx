## Why Webiny?

Webiny was created to address the growing need for a flexible, scalable, and developer-friendly content platform that can handle complex content management requirements. Traditional CMS solutions often fall short when it comes to customization, scalability, and developer experience. Webiny aims to fill this gap by providing a modern, serverless architecture that allows developers to build and manage content-driven applications with ease.

<IconTitle icon="thumb_up" title="When to use Webiny?" heading="h3" />

Webiny is an excellent choice for projects that require:
- **Scalability**: Webiny's serverless architecture allows your applications to scale automatically based on demand, making it suitable for projects with fluctuating traffic.
- **Data privacy and control**: With Webiny, you have full control over your data and infrastructure, allowing you to comply with data privacy regulations and maintain ownership of your content.
- **Customization**: Webiny's modular architecture enables developers to customize and extend the platform to meet specific project requirements.
- **Operational efficiency**: Webiny's built-in features, such as content modeling, page building, content approval workflows, and asset management, streamline content management processes and improve editorial workflows, especially within large and distributed teams.
- **Security**: Webiny's architecture is designed with security in mind, providing features such as role-based access control, folder level permissions, SOC2 compliance, SSO integration and secure content delivery.
- **Multi-tenant architecture**: Webiny supports multi-tenant setups, making it ideal for agencies or organizations managing multiple websites or applications from a single instance.
